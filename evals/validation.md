## Corrections and targeted retests

The first pass identified ambiguity about accepting provisional scopes when literature access is unavailable. The instructions now distinguish accepting a bounded working direction from validating its scientific premises. Disclosed gaps and verification tasks carry forward; an unclear or internally conflicting scope is still not ready for a decision.

A second clarification states that an unambiguous request to use a shown artifact version for the next stage can itself be acceptance. The workflow does not require a special phrase or duplicate confirmation.

Two targeted retests by the relevant evaluators confirmed:

- Explicit acceptance of provisional S1 with known source gaps unlocked question work, retained unverified literature status, and preserved the next checkpoint.
- 'Use this L3 version to write the review,' after a concrete site/corpus presentation and accepted upstream scope, counted as stage-3 acceptance without another approval question.

A final wording clarification made the library's request for acceptance conditional on it not already having been provided. It did not alter the acceptance rule; the cases were not repeated after this wording-only change.
