# Question Card

Use a compact record rather than filling every field mechanically. Preserve unknowns. Evidence and a proposed check belong in different fields.

## Main question

Record the stable question ID, priority, question-set revision, and accepted subfield revision. Distinguish a candidate from a user-accepted question. The completed set is a stage-2 checkpoint, not permission to begin later-stage artifacts.

- **Question:** In which system and conditions, what is unknown about which quantity, relationship, or construction?
- **Parent aim:** What explanation, capability, or decision depends on the answer?
- **Consequence:** What would change if the answer differed from the current expectation?
- **Scope:** What is included, excluded, and held fixed?
- **Gap status:** Unknown to the researcher; literature-unresolved within the recorded search; transfer or replication; feasibility-blocked; or a combination.
- **Current best answer:** What is already supported, with its limits?

## Prior-work boundary

Record the closest work, not just influential or broadly related papers.

| Source and stable link | What was actually examined | System, conditions, and relevant result | Exact remaining difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verified source or pending search | Full text, figure, methods, abstract, or metadata | Evidence with a location when available | Unresolved claim, transfer limit, or no remaining gap |

Add search date, venues or databases, query terms, and citation trails checked when a novelty assessment is requested. A bounded search can support "not resolved in the sources checked." It cannot establish a universal absence. Mark unavailable full text and unresolved contradictory findings.

## Definitions and inference bridges

| Construct or step | Operational meaning | Observable or proof obligation | Assumptions and failure modes | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target property | Definition independent of a convenient proxy | What could establish it | Where the mapping can fail | Known, inferred, or unknown |
| Observation to processed quantity | Transformation and calibration | Recorded data and checks | Processing artifacts or loss of information | Verified or pending |
| Processed quantity to mechanism/function | Required model or causal bridge | Additional distinguishing evidence | Alternative accounts | Supported, conditional, or unresolved |

Keep correlation, intervention, prediction, and mechanism claims distinct. Several methods may share a confound; record what independent constraint each contributes.

## Dependencies and micro-questions

Use a dependency graph or this table. Include only tasks that affect the answer or establish a necessary prerequisite.

| Micro-question | Depends on | Possible answers | Effect on the parent question | Smallest useful task |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A bounded uncertainty | Prior evidence or task ID | Include null and unresolved outcomes | Claim, branch, or decision that changes | Input, method, and output |

Separate a **scientific uncertainty** from an **execution task**. "Run an analysis" is an activity. "Can these data distinguish the two proposed explanations?" is a question that an analysis may answer.

## Alternatives and answer criteria

For explanatory work, state the competing predictions and a result that would weaken each. For other work, use the appropriate objective and acceptance criteria rather than invented rivals.

| Explanation or approach | Prediction or objective | Distinguishing evidence | What would weaken or reject it | Remaining ambiguity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Candidate account or strategy | Direction, pattern, quantitative range if justified, or proof obligation | Observation, control, computation, derivation, or counterexample | Prespecified criterion where possible | Confounds or indistinguishable cases |

Check practical as well as structural identifiability. A predicted difference smaller than attainable uncertainty may not be usable. Unknown uncertainty calls for calibration or a pilot, not an invented threshold.

## Next action and branching decision

Add an important-reading card for each article needed to understand or investigate the question: verified citation/paper ID, role, why it matters, exact inspected location and access status, experimental or analytical lesson, transferable steps, system-specific limits, and missing protocol details. Label exploratory recommendations with unverified applicability.

- **Action:** A task small enough to execute and inspect.
- **Input and capability:** Data, theory, software, instrument, expertise, or access required; distinguish available from assumed.
- **Output:** The evidence or result to retain, including provenance and analysis settings.
- **Decision criteria:** What counts as informative, null, contradictory, or inconclusive? Justify tolerances from the question and measurement capability.
- **Informative result:** Which claim or branch changes?
- **Null result:** What effect is bounded, under which precision and assumptions? A non-significant result alone does not establish absence.
- **Inconclusive result:** Which uncertainty prevents a decision, and can a different task resolve it?
- **Stop or pivot:** When would continuing no longer answer an important or tractable question?

Keep proposed studies separate from work executed within this stage. Present the whole question set for user acceptance before building the website. When results arrive in separately requested research work, update evidence and dependencies without rewriting prior predictions to match the outcome.
